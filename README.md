# Coffee_shop_sales
Retail Analytics: Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

A comprehensive data visualization project focusing on sales trends and consumer behavior.  

KPI Tracking: Total Sales ($698K+), Footfall, Avg Bill per Person, and Avg Order Size.  
Dimensional Analysis: Breakdown by Product Category, Cup Size, and Store Location (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, Lower Manhattan). 
Temporal Trends: Hourly sales heatmaps and day-of-week volume analysis to assist in staff scheduling and inventory management.
